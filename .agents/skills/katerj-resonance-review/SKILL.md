# KaterJ Resonance Review

## Outcome

Find the single belief or pain the piece should organize around and remove competing messages that dilute it.

## Workflow

1. List every claim in the draft without evaluating it.
2. Choose the one claim that still deserves the video if all others are removed.
3. Test whether the copy says something the audience recognizes but rarely states plainly.
4. Identify the viewer's practical and emotional payoff for finishing or sharing.
5. Check whose side the narrative stays on; reject shifts from ally to lecturer.
6. Identify the first person likely to share it and their reason.
7. Mark supporting claims as keep, shorten, merge, or remove.

## BoomEarth use

Store the diagnosis privately with the rewrite candidate. The public script keeps the final argument and examples, not the review vocabulary.

## Acceptance

- One core mechanism can be stated in one sentence.
- Supporting facts strengthen that mechanism rather than becoming parallel lessons.
- The viewer pain is specific enough to recognize in daily work.
- The close returns to the same promise opened at the beginning.
- The review proposes textual actions, not vague advice to “add emotion”.
