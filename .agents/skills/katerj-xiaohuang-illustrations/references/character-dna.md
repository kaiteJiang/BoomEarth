# Xiaohuang character DNA

## Invariants

- Warm yellow, asymmetric seed-shaped body; soft and handmade, never a perfect oval.
- One thin black antenna ending in a clearly hollow heart.
- Two vertical black oval eyes, a tiny curved mouth and light blush.
- Thin black arms and legs attached directly to the body.
- Friendly, curious and useful rather than childish, glossy or commercial-mascot polished.

## Variables

Pose, expression, viewpoint, scene, prop, scale and medium may change. Keep the body silhouette, hollow-heart antenna, facial proportions, limb weight and warm palette recognizable in every variation.

## Reject

Reject a pear, egg, droplet, chick, animal, generic yellow mascot, filled-heart antenna, round cartoon eyes, thick limbs, plastic 3D toy finish or a passive character standing beside unrelated text.
