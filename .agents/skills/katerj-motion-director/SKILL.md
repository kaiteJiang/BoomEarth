# KaterJ Motion Director

## Outcome

Turn an explanation into a visible sequence of state changes so the result behaves like a video rather than a deck with transitions.

## Workflow

1. State the moving thesis: object, starting state, ending state, and claim proved by the transformation.
2. Build a continuous beat graph with time, narrative job, moving object, state change, text role, asset need, and slideshow risk.
3. Choose two to four recurring motion primitives such as reveal, wipe, route, branch, transfer, compression, comparison, or settle.
4. Keep the page or scene base stable; animate meaningful components in narration order.
5. Give still images narrative movement through masks, crops, parallax layers, or state transitions.
6. Bind hero terms and reveals to final word timestamps rather than estimated scene midpoints.
7. Render a short sample when the visual language materially changes, then carry the approved grammar into `katerj-video-director`.

## Anti-slideshow gate

Reject a plan built from repeated title-and-bullet pages, identical card layouts, whole-page drifting, decorative motion with no state change, or long reading blocks. Most beats must visibly transform, travel, reveal, compare, or resolve.

## Acceptance

- Every beat has a narrative job and visible state change.
- Adjacent scenes vary their component structure.
- Text supports the motion rather than carrying the whole explanation.
- Contact-sheet and semantic-cue frames prove continuity and timing.
