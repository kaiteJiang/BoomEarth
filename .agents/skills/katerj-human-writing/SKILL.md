# KaterJ Human Writing

## Outcome

Keep the author's evidence and judgment while turning technical Chinese into direct, spoken, concrete language.

## Preserve

- Facts, dates, costs, model names, version numbers, and uncertainty.
- Specific actions: measured, downloaded, rendered, failed, fixed, verified.
- The author's real position, including limits and inconvenient trade-offs.

## Workflow

1. Separate facts, judgment, experience, and the reader's next useful action.
2. Delete ceremonial setup, repeated conclusions, vague praise, and lecture-style transitions.
3. Replace abstract labels with visible consequences: time saved, failure avoided, artifact produced, or boundary enforced.
4. Keep technical English tokens such as Agent, API, Codex, Claude Code, ASR, TTS, SHA-256, and QC when they are the clearest terms.
5. Read the copy aloud; break long syntax, restore natural uneven rhythm, and complete dangling references.
6. End on a concrete result or judgment rather than a slogan.

## Common repairs

- Replace “真正、其实、本质上、核心在于” with the claim or evidence itself.
- Use at most one useful contrast; remove repeated “不是 A 而是 B” scaffolding.
- Replace “更高级、更自然、更适合” with the exact task or comparison.
- Avoid command-template openings such as “别急着、先别、顺序别反了”.

## Acceptance

- A listener can understand every sentence on the first pass.
- Numbers and product names remain unchanged.
- The text contains project evidence rather than generic authority language.
- No new story, measurement, quotation, or personal experience was invented.
