# Closed-boundary rules

Choose boundaries in this order: semantic closure, compact reading load, protected phrases, then surface rhythm.

## Good landing points

- after a complete statement, question, action, or result;
- before a contrast or conclusion when the next clause can start independently;
- between parallel items that each carry their own meaning;
- after a short spoken lead-in such as `说白了` when a complete explanation follows.

## Boundaries to reject

- after glue words such as `的、地、得、把、被、因为、如果、虽然、为了、是否、能不能`;
- before orphaned continuations such as `是、而是、才、才能、所以、因此`;
- inside a product name, English sentence, number and unit, negation pair, four-character phrase, or core verb-object phrase;
- between a setup such as `真正重要的是` and the content that completes it.

If merging two broken lines creates an overlong line, return to an earlier clause boundary. Do not fix a dangling fragment by moving only one or two characters.

## Length contract

| Output | Preferred | Hard review |
|---|---:|---:|
| Narration / teleprompter | 8-14 CJK characters | 16 |
| Single-line caption | 6-12 CJK characters | 14 |

Punctuation, spaces, and Markdown markers do not count as Chinese characters. An indivisible proper name may exceed the threshold only when it occupies the line without a second relationship.

## English and timing

Keep an embedded English name visually separated from Chinese, and never break a complete English sentence before its terminal punctuation. With timed captions, preserve existing cues unless word-level timestamps support the new split.
