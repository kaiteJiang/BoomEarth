# KaterJ Hook Review

## Outcome

Decide whether the opening earns attention without misleading the viewer, then specify the smallest repair needed.

## Workflow

1. Read the complete body before judging the opening.
2. Check that the first spoken line identifies the topic, conflict, pain, or measurable result without relying on title or cover.
3. Identify the hook type and the proof that supports it.
4. Check six dimensions: independent meaning, attention reason, unanswered tension, credibility, speakability, and fulfillment.
5. Reject numbers, savings, speed, download counts, or performance claims that lack evidence.
6. Prefer repairing the body when the opening is strong but the script cannot fulfill it.
7. Return pass/fail, the first failing sentence, and one recommended opening.

## Failure examples

- Generic greeting before the topic.
- Asking for likes before explaining value.
- Self-question/self-answer phrasing that sounds written rather than spoken.
- Giving the full conclusion before creating any reason to continue.
- Claiming “one click, zero cost, always” without the required conditions.

## Acceptance

- The topic appears immediately.
- Pain and value are concrete and body-backed.
- CTA is one sentence after value.
- The recommended opening can be read naturally in one breath group.
