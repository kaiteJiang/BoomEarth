# KaterJ Topic Planning

## Outcome

Turn a casual idea into one traceable topic card and route every chosen content form to its own workbench without asking the user to move files.

## Boundaries

- Keep one card at `01-内容生产/00-选题池/YYYY-MM-DD-主题.md`.
- A card records the idea, judgment, safe source pointer, status, and project links; finished copy lives in a workbench.
- Source URLs and source IDs may exist only on the topic card or under the private video workbench.
- Never treat real-time popularity data as available when its reader fails.

## Workflow

1. For “记录、先存着、灵感”, create or update the topic card and stop after returning its path.
2. For “深化”, add audience pain, angle, evidence, risks, and candidate content forms without drafting the final work.
3. For “立项”, ensure the card exists, choose one or more forms, create each workbench entity, and link it back from the card.
4. Route source video to `katerj-video-wash`; source-free copy to `katerj-script-rewrite`; final video production to `katerj-video-director`.
5. For recommendations, filter recent evidence through the private wash ledger before ranking candidates.
6. On publication or revision feedback, update the publication board and project status without moving the topic card.

## Acceptance

- Exactly one topic card represents the idea.
- Every launched form has a working relative link and a clear status.
- Source information is absent from public drafts and delivery reports.
- Failed cloud or monitoring reads are reported as unavailable, never replaced by stale data.
