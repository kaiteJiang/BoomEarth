# KaterJ Video Hook

## Outcome

Write an opening that immediately names the real subject, gives the intended viewer a reason to stay, and makes only promises the body can prove.

## Hook choices

- Value: lead with a concrete result the video will teach.
- Pain: name the frustrating situation the viewer already recognizes.
- Evidence: open with a measured result, failure, or finished artifact.
- Question: use a real unresolved conflict, not a fake self-answer.
- Event: begin at a specific failure, decision, or turning point.

Choose one primary type. A second type is allowed only when it shortens the route to the subject.

## Workflow

1. Extract the audience, current pain, promised result, and strongest proof from the body.
2. Put the object, conflict, pain, or result in the first spoken sentence.
3. Follow with the viewer pain and the exact benefit this video will deliver.
4. Add one short “点赞/收藏/关注” request only after value is clear.
5. Bridge directly into the first proof segment.
6. Send the result to `katerj-hook-review` and bind title, cover copy, hook, and proof with the platform-title skill.

The first line carries the 前 3 秒 attention contract. 标题、封面字、开场钩子 must each do a different job and must not repeat word for word.

## Acceptance

- The opening works without seeing the title or cover.
- The first sentence is speakable and names a real topic.
- Numbers and time claims have project evidence.
- The body contains every promised result.
- No generic “今天介绍、太牛了、一定看到最后” opener remains.
